The dashboard is the entry point. Arrows show the main lesson-management path; the other cards are the student’s account tools.

Updates personal details, country, timezone, and learning information.

3. Public section → complete booking flow

A visitor can begin from online classes, a teacher profile, or a direct booking link. Checkout is one backend transaction: it validates the slot, charges the dummy gateway, creates the purchase/session/invoice, and creates an account only when needed.
